# Telegram GREEN-API chat
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React required by the assignment. TypeScript + Vite follow the implementation plan accepted with the user's repository handoff.

## Users
A reviewer or account owner who wants to send a text to a Telegram contact and see their reply.

## Product Purpose
Connect an authorized GREEN-API Telegram instance, create a conversation by phone number, send text and receive replies through HTTP notifications.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Text only. Minimal messenger interface. Credentials entered at runtime and held in memory. Approximately 20–30 feature/fix commits requested by the user. No real credentials supplied yet; live delivery remains unverified until an authorized instance is available.

## Brand Commitments
Messenger-style two-column layout from the assignment; Telegram replaces MAX by explicit user instruction. Russian interface, inferred from the conversation.

## Product Principles
Keep the required exchange easy to follow. Show actual connection and delivery states. Keep credentials out of durable storage. Explain recoverable errors.
